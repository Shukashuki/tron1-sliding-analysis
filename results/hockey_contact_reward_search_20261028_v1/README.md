## What the result means

Contact is checked at every 2.5 ms physical sample over the 2.76 s reference, including the terminal state. Both wheels need valid native contact geometry within the unchanged 0.1 mm separation tolerance; a single missed or unknown sample fails. Contact does not depend on a normal-force threshold, equal wheel loads or an absolute body-height target. This is sampled simulation evidence, not a claim about unobserved time between steps.

The remaining gates retain approach/entry speed, XY tracking, body/wheel heading, tilt, stopping and at least 0.05 s of corroborated lateral sliding. Sliding requires both contacts, tangential-velocity and displacement evidence, near-capacity aggregate friction and dissipative opposing forces. This is not a pointwise friction-cone certificate or an observed PhysX stick/slip flag. [Frozen independent qualifier](source_snapshot_v3/qualify_hockey_contact_search.py), [task/sliding gates](source_snapshot_v3/analyze_hockey_bilateral.py).

The frozen **stop-hold** gate means 0.5 s of bilateral contact, XY speed at most 0.1 m/s and tilt at most 30°. It does not certify that vertical motion has settled. Failed-contact tracking and sliding metrics below remain diagnostic and cannot override the primary failure. A nominal pass requires a separate 33-case initial-state robustness suite before completion can be considered.

## Contact-continuity refinement

The [contact-loss replay](contact_loss_resolution_audit_v1/report.json) and [four-column plot](contact_loss_resolution_audit_v1/contact_loss_resolution_4x1.png) isolate a remaining reward-resolution issue. The current loss averages eight physical intervals. With the existing contact weights, losing one interval can still leave a positive contact-only score; this is not a claim about the total task reward. In the dense XYZ actor, two of five affected control windows retain positive contact scores.

The [candidate](../../config/hockey_contact_continuity_v1.json) replaces only this aggregation with `0.5 * (mean(missing) + max(missing))`. Complete contact and complete loss keep their old values, while a single missed interval costs 0.5625 instead of 0.125. The existing +2 contact fraction and -4 loss weights then give a negative contact-only score for any missed interval, while retaining graded duration feedback. Stop speed, sliding, numerical weights, parent initialization, DR, sampling and independent success gates are unchanged. On the unchanged dense XYZ trajectory, the loss integral changes from -0.22 to -0.31; this counterfactual does not establish a learned improvement.

The training rule's missing duration is **55 ms**, versus **47.5 ms** in native contact qualification: training combines native pre-step contact with authored post-step clearance. These are distinct diagnostics, and the replay does not replace the frozen native-contact gate or its endpoint check.
