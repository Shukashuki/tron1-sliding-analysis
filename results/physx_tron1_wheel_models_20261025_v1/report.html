Force-application coupling control

External regularized Coulomb and nonlinear combined-slip arms use identical load/contact-point coupling. Native tangential friction is disabled in both, while native normal contact remains. Both external laws use the preceding physical sample's normal load and measured point, with2.5 ms coupling delay. Their applied forces, COM moments and dissipative contact power are independently recomputed from recorded kinematics. The first native-friction buffer precedes material replacement; every subsequent native-friction buffer is checked near zero. It is not counted as simultaneous native/external friction.

The nonlinear coefficients are sensitivity assumptions, not identified TRON1 tire parameters. This is an external wrench model, not built-in PhysX VehicleSDK. Aggregated friction patches and global-Z load do not identify pointwise friction cones. Full native normal vectors may include XY force; body β, wheel-hub angle and contact-point sliding are distinct. A larger support fraction alone is not continuous support, and no absolute-height/flight gate has been introduced.
