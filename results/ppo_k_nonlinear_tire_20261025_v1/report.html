This is a dated interim record from 2026-10-25, not a completed task result. The 32-environment native PhysX smoke and strict normalized-actor export verification passed. A new 512-environment, 12,288,000-transition policy was training when this evidence was collected. Contact continuity, tracking and full-body stability have not yet been qualified for that policy.

One declared physical-model change

The new cohort starts from the same seed-42 network as K and retains the exact K contact reward, other 14 reward terms, nonzero reference β reward, 500/10 leg PD, actuator limits, observations, reference and 400 Hz physics / 50 Hz policy clocks. Original authored wheel cylinders, mass, COM and inertia are retained. Absolute body height remains free, and single-wheel support is allowed.

PhysX still supplies normal contact. A device-batched, combined-slip force law replaces wheel-ground tangential friction. Native wheel static/dynamic coefficients are read back as zero, preventing native/external force duplication. The original per-world startup draw μ∈[0.3,1.2], shared by the wheels, supplies the external force capacity μFz. Encoder, base-COM and push randomization retain their existing settings. The protocol explicitly records this change of friction implementation.

The force uses the preceding physical sample's normal load and contact position: one 2.5 ms coupling delay. After an episode reset, the first physical application in that world is zero; another world's force/history remains intact. The curve uses B=4.5, C=1.8, E=1 and 0.2 m/s velocity regularization. These are declared sensitivity parameters, not identified TRON1 tire measurements. Static adhesion, tire elasticity and a full vehicle tire model are not claimed.
