Only14.3/8.6% exceed the descriptive0.1m/s cutoff. This shows why body-beta tracking alone is insufficient evidence of sustained wheel sliding. It does not establish that there is no within-step slip: the reported point aggregates patches,and velocity is sampled after the solver. Active leg motion,load alternation and solver impulses all matter.

For a direct sliding task,define desired loaded-wheel lateral surface velocity or wheel-local slip angle explicitly,alongside support,speed,path and body integrity. Wheel speed/torque can control longitudinal slip; lateral motion and balance also require whole-body/yaw dynamics. A force-based nonlinear tire model changes the slip-to-force relation,it does not make a desired slip an instantaneous actuator command. The current fresh K/M comparison stays frozen.
